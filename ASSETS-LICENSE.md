## Extra permission for Ship projects

A "Ship project" is Ship of Harkinian, or any software built from a copy of its source code, modified or not (for example a fork, or a port that includes Ship of Harkinian's code). Mods and resource packs made to be loaded by a Ship project also count.

Inside a Ship project, you may use, share and adapt the assets for any purpose, including commercial ones. This covers paid development, commissioned builds, bounties, donations and sponsored work, and showing a Ship project in monetized videos or streams. You must still credit the creators as CC BY requires, for example by keeping CREDITS.md.

Like the license itself, this permission can't be withdrawn later. It only applies while the assets are used as part of a Ship project. Using them anywhere else (another game, an asset store, merchandise) is covered by plain CC BY-NC 4.0 only.

Artists keep the copyright to their work. This license only gives permission to use it.

Some assets are based on Nintendo's originals. For those, these terms only cover the contributor's own changes.

By submitting assets, you confirm they are your own work (or that you have the right to release them), and you agree to release them under these terms. Once released, they stay under these terms, including outside the Harbour Masters organisation.

The license doesn't require it, but if you want to use our assets outside a Ship project, please ask the artist first. It's a small sign of respect for the people who made the work.
